# Data Analysis: Jet Airline Safety 

## Project Overview
My aviation insurance client asked me to review accident history (1983-2023) and make recommendations for the least risky makes and models to underwrite. We specifically targeted planes with the lowest rates of total destruction and serious/fatal passenger injuries.

## The Safest Bets
The data was processed to show the following when modern, professionally designed airplanes were filtered:
Large passenger jets - Boeing and Airbus are the safest manufacturers. In particular, the Boeing 737 series and the Airbus A320 family have very low serious injury rates (almost zero).
Small Aircraft: Cessna and Piper reign. Absolute standouts in preventing total loss and passenger safety are models such as the Cessna 150/152 and Piper PA-28 series.

The "X-Factors" (Other Risks)
Outside of the aircraft there are two factors that have significant effect on safety:
Heavy cloud and low visibility are much more destructive and deadly than clear weather when flying in Instrument Meteorological Conditions (IMC).
High speed / high altitude accidents (cruising and maneuvering) are very destructive, whereas landing accidents are much more survivable.
